TRANSFORMERS

Move with arrows or WASD. Jump with Space — tap again in the air for a double jump. Fire the energy blaster with F or J. Press T to transform into a truck and ram pigs. Collect power-ups. GoGo Squeezes make you invincible for 20 seconds. Type G-O-G-O quickly to activate the drone attack. Press V to turn invisible. T transforms with sound and animation. D increases speed, S decreases it. Clear the long pig run. Whale bosses spawn with extra white shield layers — climb each parkour crystal to peel a layer, then fight.
